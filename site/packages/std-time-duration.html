std/time/duration

#time

import std/time/duration

Duration, an exact signed elapsed time with private seconds and nanoseconds. Constructors from nanoseconds to hours, and from_seconds_f64. Arithmetic: plus, minus, times, divide, ratio, negate, abs, with try_ forms returning an optional. Plain forms panic on overflow. Also round and truncate with a Rounding, comparison, to_string (ISO 8601 PT1H30M), friendly (1h 30m) and parse, which reads both forms and fails at the offset of the first bad character.

Imports std/text, std/time/fault, std/time/rounding.
