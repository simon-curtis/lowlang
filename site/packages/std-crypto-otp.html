std/crypto/otp

#crypto

import std/crypto/otp

HOTP (RFC 4226) and TOTP (RFC 6238) over SHA-1, SHA-256 and SHA-512: hotp, totp, verify_totp with a window, verify_hotp, generate_secret, base32 secrets, and otpauth:// uri and parse_uri.

Imports std/base32, std/builder, std/crypto, std/crypto/hash, std/text.
