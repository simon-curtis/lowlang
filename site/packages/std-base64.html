std/base64

#encoding

import std/base64

encode and decode, and encode_url and decode_url for the URL-safe alphabet without padding (RFC 4648 §5). The URL-safe decoder rejects padding, other alphabets and non-zero trailing bits.

Functions

decode

pub fn decode(s: str) -> []u8 ! DecodeError

Decodes padded standard Base64. The length must be a multiple of 4, so unpadded input fails with BadLength. = is accepted only as the last one or two characters. Fails with BadByte at the index of the first byte outside the alphabet or of a misplaced =. Bits left over in the last character are ignored. Empty input gives empty data.

decode_url

pub fn decode_url(s: str) -> []u8 ! DecodeError

Decodes unpadded URL-safe Base64. =, + and / are rejected. Fails with BadLength when the length is 1 modulo 4, and with BadByte at the index of the first byte outside the alphabet. Non-zero bits left over in the last character fail with BadByte at the final index.

encode

pub fn encode(data: []u8) -> str

Returns data encoded as standard Base64 (RFC 4648 section 4), padded with = to a multiple of 4 characters.

encode_url

pub fn encode_url(data: []u8) -> str

Returns data encoded as URL-safe Base64 (RFC 4648 section 5) without padding. The alphabet uses - and _ in place of + and /.
