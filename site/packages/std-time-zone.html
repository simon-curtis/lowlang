std/time/zone

#time

import std/time/zone

IANA zones. Zone is a shared value decoded once, from named (the host database, honouring ZONEINFO), load_from (any fn(str) -> []u8?), local (TZ, /etc/localtime, /etc/timezone, then UTC), from_posix, decode, utc or fixed. Queries: info_at, candidates (One, Overlap, Gap for a civil time), next_transition, previous_transition and transitions.

Imports std/file, std/io, std/os, std/text, std/time/fault, std/time/offset, std/time/timestamp.
