std/webauthn

#crypto #web

import std/webauthn

A WebAuthn relying party: registration and authentication options as JSON, registration and authentication verification for ES256, EdDSA and RS256 credentials, and COSE key parsing.

Imports std/base64, std/cbor, std/crypto, std/crypto/ecdsa, std/crypto/ed25519, std/crypto/hash, std/crypto/rsa, std/json.
