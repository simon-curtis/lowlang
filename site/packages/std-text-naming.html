parts

pub fn parts(s: str) -> []str

Splits an identifier or phrase into its words, keeping their original case. Words break at any character that is not a letter, number or mark, at a change from a lowercase to an uppercase letter, and between letters and digits. An uppercase run followed by a lowercase letter ends before that letter, and a plural s stays with the run before it. Apostrophes are dropped and combining marks stay with the letter before them. So parseHTTPResponse gives parse, HTTP, Response, user_id2 gives user, id, 2, and APIs stays whole. Text with no letters or digits gives an empty slice.

slug_with

pub fn slug_with(s: str, o: SlugOptions) -> str

Returns s as a lowercase ASCII slug made of its words joined by o.separator. The text is decomposed with NFKD and combining marks, apostrophes and quotes inside words are dropped, so Café gives cafe. Letters with no decomposition are spelled out where there is a convention, such as ß as ss, æ as ae, ø as o, đ as d, þ as th and ŋ as ng. Every other character, including letters of scripts that have no ASCII form, ends the current word, and runs of them do not repeat the separator. Returns an empty string when no ASCII letter or digit remains.
