std/time/span

#time

import std/time/span

Span, a calendar amount of years, months, weeks, days and time units with one sign. Unit, Diff (largest, smallest, increment, mode), to_duration and from_duration, ISO 8601 to_string (P1Y2M3DT4H), friendly and parse.

Imports std/text, std/time/duration, std/time/fault, std/time/rounding.
