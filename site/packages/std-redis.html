std/redis

#database #net

import std/redis

RESP2 and RESP3 over TCP or TLS: a droppable Client with a ClientHandle, connect with AUTH, SELECT and HELLO 3, command, typed helpers for strings, hashes, lists, sets, sorted sets and publish, pipelining, transaction, pub/sub through a channel usable in select, command timeouts, and limits on bulk size and nesting.

Imports std/io, std/net, std/sync, std/text, std/tls.
