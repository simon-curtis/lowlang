std/term

#os

import std/term

is_tty, size and set_size. enter_raw returns a droppable Raw that restores the terminal when released. Key, read_key (a lone escape resolves after 50 ms) and parse_keys. ANSI helpers: fg, bg with 16, 256 and RGB colours, bold, dim, underline, cursor movement, clear_screen, the alternate screen, strip and visible_width. colour_enabled honours NO_COLOR, CLICOLOR_FORCE and TERM=dumb. open_pty.

Imports std/io, std/os, std/text.
