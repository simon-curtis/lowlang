std/sql/pool

#database

import std/sql/pool

A pool of connections made by an opener. open takes a Config (max, max_idle, idle_timeout, max_lifetime, wait_timeout, ping_after, ping, clock). acquire and acquire_until (with a cancel token) park in first-in first-out order while every connection is in use. A droppable Lease returns its connection on release. Also with, discard, prune, stats and shutdown.

Imports std/cancel, std/sql, std/sync, std/text, std/time/clock, std/time/duration.
