std/time/datetime

#time

import std/time/datetime

DateTime, a civil date and time of day with no zone: plus by Span or Duration, until and since with a Diff, round, truncate, to_string, parse (an offset is rejected), and pattern format and parse_with.

Imports std/text, std/time/date, std/time/duration, std/time/fault, std/time/pattern, std/time/rounding, std/time/span, std/time/timeofday.

DateTime

pub struct DateTime {
    ...
}

A date and a time of day on a wall clock, with no offset or zone.

It names a local time, not an instant. Arithmetic treats every day as exactly 24 hours, so it never accounts for daylight saving changes. Use zoned for that. A DateTime is a date.Date from -9999-01-01 to 9999-12-31 with a timeofday.TimeOfDay.

pub fn civil_seconds(dt: DateTime) -> i64

Returns the whole seconds from 1970-01-01T00:00:00 to dt, counting the local time as if it were UTC.

The fraction of a second is dropped, and the result is negative before the epoch.

plus

pub fn plus(dt: DateTime, s: span.Span) -> DateTime ! fault.Fault

Returns dt moved forward by the span s.

The years, months, weeks and days are added to the date as date.plus does, with the day moved back to the last day of a shorter month. The time units are then added as an exact duration, carrying into the date. A negative span moves backwards. Fails with OutOfRange when the result is outside the supported years and with Overflow when the time units do not fit in a Duration.

plus_duration

pub fn plus_duration(dt: DateTime, d: duration.Duration) -> DateTime ! fault.Fault

Returns the date-time d after dt, counting every day as 24 hours.

A negative d moves backwards. Fails with Overflow when the sum does not fit in a Duration and with OutOfRange when the result is outside the supported years.

round

pub fn round(dt: DateTime, unit: span.Unit, increment: i64, mode: rounding.Rounding) -> DateTime ! fault.Fault

Returns dt with its time of day rounded to a multiple of increment units since midnight using mode.

unit can be Days, which needs an increment of 1 and rounds to a midnight. A result that reaches the end of the day becomes 00:00 on the next day. Fails with Invalid when unit is a larger calendar unit or the increment does not divide the next larger unit as timeofday.rounded_nanos states, and with OutOfRange when rounding up passes the last supported date.

until

pub fn until(a: DateTime, b: DateTime, diff: span.Diff) -> span.Span ! fault.Fault

Returns the span from a to b, measured and rounded as diff says.

The span is negative when b is earlier. When diff.largest is a calendar unit, years and months are counted on the calendar from a and the remainder is split into weeks, days and time units. Otherwise the span is the exact duration split into time units from largest down. Fails with Invalid when the options are inconsistent as span.check_diff and span.check_time_increment state, and with OutOfRange or Overflow where the result cannot be represented.

parse

pub fn parse(s: str) -> DateTime ! fault.Fault

Parses a date and a time of day separated by T, t or a space, such as 2024-03-01T12:30:00.

The date accepts the forms of date.parse and the time the forms of timeofday.parse. Fails with Syntax at the offset of the first bad byte, including when there is no separator, and otherwise as those do.
