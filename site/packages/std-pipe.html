std/pipe

#concurrency #io

import std/pipe

Pipelines. A Pipe owns a Reader and a Writer, each droppable so that releasing it completes it, with copyable ReaderHandle and WriterHandle views for green threads (adopt_reader, adopt_writer and the _on operations). Also segmented buffers, backpressure, Sequence and SequenceReader, and adapters between pipes and streams.

Imports std/io, std/sync.

advance_to_on

pub fn advance_to_on(h: ReaderHandle, consumed: i64, examined: i64)

Reports how much of the data returned by reading was consumed and how much was examined.

Both arguments are stream offsets, the same as Sequence.start and Sequence.end. Bytes before consumed are released. The next read_on parks until data beyond examined arrives, so a parser that needs more data than it has passes the end of the buffer as examined. consumed cannot go backwards or past the flushed data, and examined is at least consumed. Wakes a parked writer when fewer than resume bytes remain unconsumed.

complete_reader_on

pub fn complete_reader_on(h: ReaderHandle)

Finishes the reading end of the pipe.

Wakes a parked writer, whose flush reports completed, and a waiting read_on, which fails with NotConnected. Later calls do nothing.

read_on

pub fn read_on(h: ReaderHandle) -> ReadResult ! io.Error

Waits until the writer has flushed bytes beyond the examined position, finished, or the read is cancelled, and returns everything not yet consumed.

Parks the green thread while it waits. Call advance_to_on afterwards to say how much was consumed and examined, otherwise the next call returns the same bytes at once. Fails with NotConnected after the reader has completed, and with the writer's error from fail_writer_on once no flushed bytes remain.

A read-only view of a range of the bytes that have gone through a pipe, which can span several buffer segments.

Offsets are positions in the whole stream, counted from the first byte written. A sequence shares the pipe's buffer segments and does not copy them, and it keeps them alive after the reader has consumed past them.

complete_writer_on

pub fn complete_writer_on(h: WriterHandle)

Finishes the writing end of the pipe.

Makes the bytes written so far readable and wakes the reader. The reader consumes what remains and then sees completed. Later calls do nothing.

fail_writer_on

pub fn fail_writer_on(h: WriterHandle, e: io.Error)

Finishes the writing end of the pipe with the error e.

Bytes written but not yet flushed are discarded. The reader consumes the bytes already flushed, and then its read fails with e. Does nothing if the writer has already finished.

flush_on

pub fn flush_on(h: WriterHandle) -> FlushResult

Makes every byte written so far readable, wakes a waiting reader, and parks the green thread while the reader is too far behind.

The writer parks while the reader has pause or more unconsumed bytes, until the reader consumes down to resume, completes, starts waiting for data or the flush is cancelled. The result reports whether the reader or the writer has completed, and whether cancel_pending_flush_on ended the flush.

get_memory_on

pub fn get_memory_on(h: WriterHandle, hint: i64) -> []u8

Returns a slice of at least hint bytes, or at least one when hint is less than 1, to write into.

Reuses the free space of the last segment when it is large enough and otherwise allocates a segment of hint bytes or segment_size, whichever is larger. The slice may be longer than hint. Call advance_on with the number of bytes filled; the bytes are visible to the reader only after flush_on. Does not park.

duplex

pub fn duplex(s: io.Stream, options: Options) -> Duplex

Returns a Duplex that reads from and writes to s.

Combines from_stream and to_stream on the same stream, so s is closed once the output is completed.

from_stream

pub fn from_stream(s: io.Stream, options: Options) -> Reader

Returns a Reader that delivers the bytes read from s.

Starts a green thread that reads s into the pipe and flushes after every read. At end of input the pipe's writer completes, and a read error fails the reader's read with that error. The green thread stops once a flush reports that the reader has completed. It never closes s.

to_stream

pub fn to_stream(s: io.Stream, options: Options) -> Writer

Returns a Writer whose flushed bytes are written to s.

Starts a green thread that writes the flushed bytes to s. It closes s when the writer completes or fails, or when a write to s fails, and then completes the reading end so the writer's flush reports completed.
