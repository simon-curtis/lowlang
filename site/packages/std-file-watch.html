std/file/watch

#io #os

import std/file/watch

inotify. watch and watch_with take paths and Options (recursive, access) and return a droppable Watcher with a copyable WatcherHandle. On a watcher: add, remove, next, events and stop. next parks the green thread until an Event (watched, name, path, kind, dir, cookie) arrives. events is a chan[Event] fed by a forwarding green thread. Kind is Created, Modified, Deleted, MovedFrom, MovedTo, Attrib, Closed, ClosedUnmodified, Opened, Accessed, SelfDeleted, SelfMoved or Overflow.

Imports std/file, std/io, std/text.
