std/hash

#crypto

import std/hash

One-shot and incremental forms of CRC-32, CRC-32C, Adler-32, CRC-64 and xxHash32/64. Also FNV-1 and FNV-1a in 32 and 64 bits; SipHash-2-4 and SipHash-1-3 with a 16-byte key in 64- and 128-bit forms; MurmurHash3 (murmur3_32, murmur3_128); wyhash final 4.2; jump consistent hashing; and rendezvous hashing.
