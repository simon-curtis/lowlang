std/time/timestamp

#time

import std/time/timestamp

Timestamp, a point on the UTC timeline from 0001-01-01 to 9999-12-31 with nanosecond precision: now, from_unix in seconds, milliseconds or nanoseconds, plus and minus by Duration, until and since, round, truncate, RFC 3339 to_string and parse (offset required, :60 read as :59), and to_http_date and parse_http_date (IMF-fixdate, RFC 850 and asctime).

Imports std/math, std/slices, std/text, std/time, std/time/calendar, std/time/duration, std/time/fault, std/time/offset, std/time/rounding, std/time/scan.

Types

Timestamp

pub struct Timestamp {
    ...
}

A point on the UTC timeline with nanosecond precision, from 0001-01-01T00:00:00Z to 9999-12-31T23:59:59.999999999Z.

It is held as seconds since the Unix epoch and a nanosecond part that is never negative, so instants before the epoch also have a nanosecond part counted forward.

round

pub fn round(ts: Timestamp, unit: duration.Duration, mode: rounding.Rounding) -> Timestamp ! fault.Fault

Returns ts rounded to a multiple of unit counted from the Unix epoch, using mode.

Fails with Invalid when unit is zero or negative, with OutOfRange when unit is longer than about 146 years, and with Overflow when the rounded result leaves the supported years.

to_string

pub fn to_string(ts: Timestamp) -> str

Returns ts in RFC 3339 form in UTC, such as 2024-03-01T12:30:00Z.

The fraction is left out when it is zero and otherwise has 3, 6 or 9 digits, whichever is the fewest that is exact.

truncate

pub fn truncate(ts: Timestamp, unit: duration.Duration) -> Timestamp ! fault.Fault

Returns ts rounded toward zero to a multiple of unit counted from the Unix epoch.

For an instant before the epoch this rounds toward the epoch, which is later. Fails in the same cases as round.

parse

pub fn parse(s: str) -> Timestamp ! fault.Fault

Parses an RFC 3339 date and time into a Timestamp.

The form is YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ss, an optional fraction of at most nine digits, and a required offset that is Z, z or a sign followed by hh, hh:mm or hh:mm:ss. The T can also be t or a space. A second of 60 is read as 59. The result is converted to UTC. Fails with Syntax at the offset of the first bad byte, with OutOfRange when a field or the offset is out of range or the result is outside the supported years, and with Invalid when the day does not exist in the month.

parse_http_date

pub fn parse_http_date(s: str) -> Timestamp ! fault.Fault

Parses an HTTP date in the IMF-fixdate form (Fri, 01 Mar 2024 12:30:00 GMT), the RFC 850 form (Friday, 01-Mar-24 12:30:00 GMT) or the asctime form (Fri Mar 1 12:30:00 2024).

The zone must be GMT where the form has one. A two-digit RFC 850 year of 69 to 99 is in the 1900s and any other is in the 2000s. Fails with Invalid when the weekday does not match the date, with Syntax at the offset of the first bad byte, and with OutOfRange when a field is out of range.
