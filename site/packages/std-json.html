std/json

#formats

import std/json

A reader and writer over UTF-8 bytes, the Value document model, equal (compares numbers numerically and objects without regard to member order), and serialize[T] and deserialize[T] with naming policies.

Imports std/base64, std/serde, std/simd, std/text.
