std/time/pattern

#time

import std/time/pattern

Compiled strftime and strptime patterns shared by the types above: compile, Names for other languages, render and scan. Verbs: %Y %y %C %m %d %e %j %b %B %a %A %u %w %U %W %V %G %H %I %M %S %f %N %p %P %z %:z %::z %Z %s %F %T %R %D %% %n %t. Flags: -, _, 0 and ^. Parsing is strict, and every error carries a byte offset.

Imports std/builder, std/text, std/time/fault.
