std/archive/cpio

#compression

import std/archive/cpio

newc, CRC and odc archives, detected per entry on read: a Reader, a Writer (add_file, add_dir, add_symlink, add_links, finish), and extract and create over the filesystem with the safe extraction of std/archive. Hard links store their data with the last link in newc and CRC. The binary format fails with Unsupported.

Imports std/archive, std/file, std/io, std/slices.
