std/slices

#data

import std/slices

Queries and transforms: contains, index, clone, concat, reverse, map, filter, fold, any, all, sum, min and max (as optionals), sorted. Sorting: sort and sort_by are unstable and in place, a quicksort with median-of-three pivots that recurses into the smaller side and finishes runs of 16 or fewer with insertion sort. sort_stable and sort_stable_by are a top-down merge sort; sort_by_key is stable. Also reduce, count, find, find_index, first, last, equal, compare, starts_with, ends_with, unique (adjacent duplicates), flat_map, flatten, repeat, lower_bound, binary_search (the first match) and binary_search_by. take, skip, chunks and windows share the source's elements. partition and zip work over Pair[A, B]. fill, swap, rotate, insert and remove are in place. Slices are not named types, so these are called with a pipe: xs \|> slices.sort().
