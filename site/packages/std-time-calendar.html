std/time/calendar

#time

import std/time/calendar

The proleptic Gregorian calendar the date types share: leap_year, days_in_month, days_from_civil and civil_from_days from the Unix epoch, weekday_index, and the English month and weekday names, full and short.

Imports std/math.

civil_from_days

pub fn civil_from_days(days: i64) -> Civil

Returns the date that is days days after 1970-01-01, or before it when days is negative.

days_from_civil

pub fn days_from_civil(year: i64, month: i64, day: i64) -> i64

Returns the number of days from 1970-01-01 to the given date, negative before it.

The month and day are not range checked.
