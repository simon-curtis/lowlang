std/rand

#crypto

import std/rand

A seedable, non-cryptographic xoshiro256** generator: seeded, rng (all 256 bits of state from std/crypto), next, unbiased below and between, float, chance, shuffle and pick. pcg32, pcg64 and chacha (seeded from 32 bytes; chacha_rng seeds from entropy) produce an Rng that every function accepts, and next32 draws 32 bits. Distributions: normal, exponential, gamma, beta, poisson, binomial, geometric, zipf, weighted_pick, cumulative with pick_cumulative, sample and permutation. Use std/crypto for secrets.

Imports std/crypto.
