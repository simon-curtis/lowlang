std/smtp

#net

import std/smtp

An SMTP client: connect, connect_starttls and connect_tls, EHLO extensions, AUTH PLAIN, LOGIN, XOAUTH2 and CRAM-MD5 (plaintext authentication is refused unless insecure_auth), send with per-recipient results, dot-stuffing, pipelining when advertised, and errors separating 4xx from 5xx.

Imports std/base64, std/crypto/hash, std/crypto/md5, std/io, std/mail, std/net, std/sync, std/text, std/tls.
