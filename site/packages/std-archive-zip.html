std/archive/zip

#compression

import std/archive/zip

Zip archives read through the central directory, with zip64, stored and deflate entries, CRC-32 checks and limits on entries, sizes and compression ratio. Written with add_file, add_dir, add_symlink and finish, which emits zip64 records when a count or size needs them. extract works over the filesystem. Encrypted entries and other methods fail with Unsupported.

Imports std/archive, std/compress/deflate, std/hash, std/io, std/slices, std/text, std/time/date, std/time/datetime.
