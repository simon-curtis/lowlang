std/time/timeofday

#time

import std/time/timeofday

TimeOfDay to the nanosecond. plus fails past midnight and plus_wrapping wraps. Also until, round, truncate, to_string, parse, and pattern format and parse_with.

Imports std/text, std/time/duration, std/time/fault, std/time/pattern, std/time/rounding, std/time/scan, std/time/span.

Types

TimeOfDay

pub struct TimeOfDay {
    ...
}

A time on a 24-hour clock with nanosecond precision, from 00:00 to 23:59:59.999999999.

It has no date, offset or zone and is held as nanoseconds since midnight.

rounded_nanos

pub fn rounded_nanos(t: TimeOfDay, unit: span.Unit, increment: i64, mode: rounding.Rounding) -> i64 ! fault.Fault

Returns t rounded to a multiple of increment units since midnight, as nanoseconds since midnight.

The result can equal a full day when t rounds up past the end of the day. Fails with Invalid when unit is a calendar unit or increment is below 1 or does not divide the next larger unit evenly (24 for hours, 60 for minutes and seconds, 1000 for the smaller units), as span.check_time_increment states.

pub fn from_fields(f: pattern.Fields) -> TimeOfDay ! fault.Fault

Returns the time of day in f.

The hour comes from hour or from hour12 with period, and the minute, second and nanosecond default to 0. Fails with Invalid when the fields do not give an hour or contradict each other, and with OutOfRange when a value is out of range.

parse

pub fn parse(s: str) -> TimeOfDay ! fault.Fault

Parses a time written as hh, hh:mm, hh:mm:ss, hhmm or hhmmss, with an optional fraction of at most nine digits after the seconds.

The text can start with T or t. A second of 60 is read as 59. Fails with Syntax at the offset of the first bad byte and with OutOfRange when a field is out of range.

Parses s with the compiled pattern p and the words of names.

Fails with Invalid when p uses a date, offset, zone or Unix-time verb, with Syntax or OutOfRange when s does not match, and with Invalid when the fields conflict or give no hour.
