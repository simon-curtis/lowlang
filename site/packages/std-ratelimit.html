std/ratelimit

#concurrency

import std/ratelimit

Bucket (token bucket in exact nanosecond units) with allow, allow_n, reserve, wait and set_rate. Window, a sliding log. Keyed, a bounded per-key set of buckets with idle eviction. All take a clock.Clock.

Imports std/cancel, std/sync, std/time/clock, std/time/duration.
