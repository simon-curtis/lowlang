std/multipart

#web

import std/multipart

multipart parsing and writing (RFC 7578, RFC 2046): a streaming Reader with limits and a spill sink for large parts, RFC 2231 and 5987 parameters, Writer, Form, and request_form for an http.Request.

Imports std/crypto, std/http, std/io, std/text.
